Closing the gap in Cancer Awareness in Africa

We’re on a mission to make early detection the norm, not the exception. Through education, screening advocacy, and community outreach, we’re helping people recognize the signs of Breast, Cervical and Prostate Cancer before it’s too late.

We are a team of health advocates, survivors and volunteers, committed to closing the awareness gap around cancer in our communities. We believe knowledge saves lives — and that no one should face a diagnosis without the information they need to catch it early.

Meet Our Teams

Increase early detection through free/low cot screening drives; Educate communities on risk factors and warning signs; Break the stigma and silence around cancer especially cervical and prostate cancer.
